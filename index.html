Space weather & propagation ☀️

The Sun decides who you can hear. Here's how to read the conditions.

—
Solar Flux (SFI)

Higher = better high-band DX. ~70 quiet, 150+ excellent.

—
K-index

Geomagnetic activity. 0–2 calm, 5+ = storms that wreck HF.

—
A-index

A daily roll-up of geomagnetic activity. Lower is better.

Get on the air: your first license

In the US it's three levels. You can start today.

  1. 1

    Study Technician

    The entry class. ~35 questions from a public pool. Free apps and sites like HamStudy make it painless.

  2. 2

    Take the exam

    Volunteer Examiners (VEs) run in-person and online sessions. Pass 26/35 and you're in. No Morse code required anymore!

  3. 3

    Get your callsign & upgrade

    The FCC issues your unique callsign in days. Later, upgrade to General and Amateur Extra for more privileges.

73 and good luck! ("73" is ham shorthand for "best regards.") Welcome to the world's greatest technical hobby.
